## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS — chosen for reliability, fast loading, and ease of maintenance for a local business website that needs to work across all devices without complex infrastructure.

## Users

**Primary users:**
- Local residents and professionals in Dehri-on-Sone seeking grooming, styling, and beauty services
- Working professionals and business people needing executive grooming and presentation services
- Brides, grooms, and families preparing for weddings and special events
- Aspiring beauty professionals and entrepreneurs looking for cosmetology training

**What they're doing:**
- Evaluating the salon's quality and services before visiting
- Booking appointments for haircuts, makeup, or treatments
- Viewing portfolio work to assess capability for important events
- Learning about academy courses and enrollment

## Product Purpose

New Look by Suruchi's website helps local clients discover and choose the salon for their grooming, styling, and training needs. Success means visitors can quickly assess service quality through portfolio work, understand what's offered across the salon/studio/academy, and take action to book or inquire.

The site serves three interconnected purposes:
1. **Client acquisition** — attract walk-ins and bookings for everyday grooming and special occasion services
2. **Portfolio showcase** — demonstrate HD makeup, bridal work, and transformation results to win high-value event clients
3. **Academy enrollment** — recruit and inform students about professional cosmetology training programs

## Positioning

**New Look by Suruchi is the only integrated salon-studio-academy in Dehri-on-Sone** — combining everyday unisex grooming, advanced bridal/event makeup artistry, and professional cosmetology training under one roof with strict hygiene standards and owner-led consultations.

Unlike conventional neighborhood parlors that focus on basic services, or specialized bridal studios that don't train, New Look offers:
- **Unisex services** for individuals, couples, and families in one visit
- **8+ years** of established local reputation (4.7–4.8 rating, verified client reviews)
- **Clean, modern environment** with sanitized tools and dedicated stations
- **Personalized styling** matched to face shapes, textures, and skin tones by Suruchi and trained team (including Sameer)
- **Career pathway** for those wanting to enter the beauty industry professionally

## Product Principles

1. **Local trust through verified quality** — leverage real portfolio work, established ratings, and owner-led reputation to convert online discovery into in-person bookings.

2. **Clear paths for three audiences** — separate but connected journeys for everyday grooming clients, special event clients, and academy students without confusion.

3. **Hygiene and professionalism as differentiators** — emphasize the clean, modern, sanitized environment that sets New Look apart from conventional local parlors.

4. **Accessibility for all** — unisex services and family-friendly positioning mean the site must welcome everyone equally, from corporate professionals to brides to aspiring beauty entrepreneurs.

5. **Mobile-first local discovery** — optimize for the phone-based search and map-driven discovery pattern of local service businesses in the region.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Must work for users with varying levels of digital literacy (simple navigation, clear calls-to-action)
- Readable text hierarchy for users viewing on small screens in bright outdoor light
- Unisex and inclusive language throughout (avoid gendered assumptions about who uses which services)
